Servus! I'm Simon, a Computer Science Masterstudent at the
Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg. Before that, I earned my
Bachelor of Science (grade 1.3, top of my class) from DHBW Stuttgart Campus
Horb.

I've been working as a Software Engineer at [DATEV eG](https://www.datev.de)
in Nürnberg since 2022, first as a dual student, now as a working student.
There I do fullstack development with Spring Boot and Angular, and I
sometimes dive into performance optimizations of our services. I also help
other developers get started with cloud development and AI-driven
engineering.

My bachelor's thesis was about optimizing the startup and response times of
Spring Boot services in the cloud using modern JVM technologies.

## Projects I'm proud of

- **VesSkel**: an open-source toolkit for fast and reproducible
  skeletonization and graph-based phenotyping of retinal fundus images.
  Published on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/vesskel/) with a napari
  plugin and a CLI. ([GitHub](https://github.com/404Simon/VesSkel))
- **TheGreenEpoch**: a simulation of CO2-aware LLM training that shows how
  much carbon you can save by pausing training when the grid's CO2 intensity
  is high. ([GitHub](https://github.com/404Simon/TheGreenEpoch))
- **AUD RAG**: a specialized chatbot for the "Algorithmen und
  Datenstrukturen" lecture, built with Laravel and retrieval-augmented
  generation. ([GitHub](https://github.com/404Simon/aud-rag))
